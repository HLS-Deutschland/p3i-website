The development gap

The opportunity is often not the problem. Converting it into an executable project is.

An opportunity, a policy ambition, a market position or a technical concept becomes actionable only when governance, data, commercial logic, technical dependencies and operating processes are aligned. P3I builds this missing decision and operating layer.

The name

P3I: Problem, Package, Process, Information.

Problem

Every initiative starts with a problem or an opportunity that is not yet clearly stated. We define it, so everyone works on the same thing.

Package

We structure it into something that can be assessed: scope, evidence, responsibilities and commercial logic.

Process

We design how the package is carried out, with governance, steps and owners, so it can move from plan to execution.

Information

Along the way the work produces prepared, checked and combined information that people can trust and act on.

Prepared information is what the system provides. Experience and thinking around the corner are what people add. Together they make Symbiotic Intelligence, the idea behind our domain, .si.

Approach

Five steps from idea to operation.

  1. IdentifyClarify the opportunity, strategic value, demand case, key constraints and critical dependencies.
    You get: a clear statement of the problem or opportunity, and whether it is worth pursuing.
  2. StructureDefine governance, commercial logic, roles, workstreams, interfaces and the project architecture.
    You get: a defined project with scope, owners and commercial logic.
  3. DigitiseCreate structured project data, auditable records, workflow controls and decision-grade information.
    You get: one set of checked information everyone works from.
  4. De-riskMap technical, regulatory, infrastructure, commercial, ESG and operational risks and convert them into actions.
    You get: a list of risks, each with an action and an owner.
  5. OperationaliseTranslate the architecture into implementation-ready workflows, responsibilities, controls, KPIs and operating interfaces.
    You get: a plan you can run, with named responsibilities and measures.

A decision environment is the combination of data, rules, processes, analytical tools and governance structures that allows a government, project sponsor or operator to make faster, evidence-based and auditable decisions. The purpose is not digitalisation for its own sake.

P3I Intelligence

The analytical layer behind project preparation.

It structures project information, measures readiness, highlights risks, tests scenarios and supports operational and strategic decisions.

Project Readiness

Maturity across land, permits, infrastructure, commercial structure, governance, ESG, logistics, operating readiness and data quality — what is known, what is missing, what must happen next.

Scenario Engine

Testing the effect of changing assumptions: CAPEX, OPEX, input and market prices, FX, infrastructure delays, energy availability, logistics constraints, local-content requirements and demand.

Document Intelligence

Permits, studies, contracts, correspondence, maps and reports structured into controlled, searchable and auditable project intelligence.

Sector Intelligence

Market, resource and infrastructure context combined into structured evidence for prioritisation and planning — in whichever sector the project sits.

Governance by design. Data ownership remains with the client or sovereign authority. Role-based access separates ministry, regulator and operator. Audit logs and segregation of duties support accountability. Open data standards are preferred to reduce vendor lock-in. AI outputs support decisions rather than replace accountable decision-makers.
